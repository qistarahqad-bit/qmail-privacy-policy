1. Who we are

Qmail for Android (“Qmail”, “the app”) is published under the Qistarah Tech brand (“Qistarah Tech”, “we”, “us”). Our office is at Apt B/5, Level 05, House 33, Road 1/A, Block J, Baridhara, Dhaka – 1212, Bangladesh.

This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used and stored, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at info@qistarah.com.

2. What Qmail is and who it is for

Qmail is an email app for authorized Qistarah Tech employees. It connects only to Qistarah Tech’s mail server, mx2.trackitbd.email, which is owned by Qistarah Tech. You sign in with the work email address and password that Qistarah Tech has given you. Qmail does not create email accounts and cannot be used with other email providers.

This policy describes how the Qmail app handles information. Section 8 explains how mail on the server relates to this policy.

4. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect to the mail server and to sync when a network is available.
  • Contacts (optional): to suggest recipients and show contact names and photos, on your device only. The app does not change your contacts; “Add to contacts” opens your contacts app. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in Android settings.
  • Notifications (optional): to tell you about new mail and problems with your account.
  • Background sync: running a background service, starting after the device restarts, scheduling exact alarms, keeping the device awake during sync and reading Android’s auto-sync setting, so that new mail arrives on time (Instant Sync).
  • Vibration: for notification alerts.
  • Fingerprint or screen lock: if you choose to reveal a saved password in the server settings, Android may ask you to confirm your identity. Android performs this check; the app receives only the result, never your biometric data.
  • Camera: the app declares this permission for a QR-code settings import that is not available in Qmail. The app does not use the camera.

5. Network connections

  • Qmail connects to mx2.trackitbd.email to receive mail (IMAP, port 993) and to send mail (SMTP, port 465).
  • Images embedded in emails are not loaded by default. If you choose to show them, they are loaded from the internet. The servers that host the images can then see your device’s IP address and that the message was opened. Images served over plain http:// are loaded without encryption.
  • Links in emails open in another app, such as your browser, only when you tap them.
  • If you set up OpenPGP, the app exchanges data with the separate OpenPGP app that you installed and selected.

6. Security

  • Both connections to the mail server are encrypted with TLS by default.
  • The outgoing mail (SMTP) connection checks the mail server’s security certificate against the certificates trusted by your device.

Current limitation: as of the effective date of this policy, the incoming mail (IMAP) connection does not verify the mail server’s security certificate. The connection is still encrypted, but the app cannot detect someone on the network who impersonates the mail server, who could then read the password and mail sent over that connection. We will update the app and this policy when certificate verification is in place. Until then, avoid using Qmail on networks you do not trust.

  • If you change the outgoing server settings yourself, the app allows you to accept a certificate that it cannot verify, or to choose an unencrypted connection. Do not do this unless Qistarah Tech’s IT staff ask you to.
  • Qmail does not provide end-to-end encryption. End-to-end encryption is available only if you set up OpenPGP with a separate OpenPGP app.
  • Your password, settings and downloaded mail are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not add its own encryption to this data; it relies on Android’s app sandbox and on device encryption where available. Protect your device with a screen lock.
  • Android cloud backup of the app’s data is turned off. Some Android devices can still copy app data directly to a new device during device-to-device transfer.

7. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your information. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not share information with third parties for advertising or analytics.

Information leaves the app only:

  • to Qistarah Tech’s mail server, as described in sections 3 and 5;
  • to the recipients of the messages you send;
  • to the servers that host remote images, if you choose to show them; and
  • to other apps when you choose, for example when you open or share an attachment or a link, export a file, or use OpenPGP.

8. Mail on the server

Your work mailbox is stored on Qistarah Tech’s mail server, not in the app. The app keeps copies of the mail it downloads, but the mailbox on the server remains the main record of your work email.

How mail is stored, protected, backed up and retained on the server is not determined by the Qmail app. It is managed by Qistarah Tech as part of its work email service and is subject to Qistarah Tech’s internal policies. This policy does not state a fixed retention period or backup schedule for the server. For information about how your mailbox is handled on the server, contact info@qistarah.com.

When your employment or authorization ends, Qistarah Tech may disable or remove your work email account. The app then can no longer connect to it. Mail that the app has already downloaded stays on your device until you remove the account from the app or uninstall the app.

9. Retention and deletion

On your device

  • Downloaded mail, attachments, your password and your settings stay on your device until you remove the account from the app or uninstall the app.
  • Removing an account from Qmail deletes that account’s downloaded mail, attachments, password, settings and account picture from your device.
  • Uninstalling Qmail deletes all of the app’s data from your device.
  • Files you have exported to other locations, such as settings exports (which never include passwords) or exported logs, are not deleted by the app. You can delete them yourself.

On the mail server

  • Removing an account from the app does not delete your mailbox or any messages stored on the mail server.
  • When you delete a message in Qmail, the app asks the server to delete it according to your account’s settings, for example by first moving it to the Trash folder.
  • Retention and deletion of mail on the server, including any server backups, are handled by Qistarah Tech as described in section 8. To ask about deleting server-side data, contact info@qistarah.com.

10. Diagnostic logs

  • Diagnostic logging is off by default.
  • If you turn on debug logging, the app writes diagnostic messages to the device log. If you turn on sync debug logging, it also writes a log file to its private storage.
  • Logs stay on your device. They leave the device only if you choose to export them and then share the exported file.
  • If you also turn on “sensitive logging”, logs can contain account details, such as your account name, and server communication details. Turn it off when you no longer need it, and check exported logs before sharing them.

11. Children

Qmail is intended only for authorized Qistarah Tech employees. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through the app.

12. Your choices and requests

  • You can refuse or withdraw the Contacts and Notifications permissions at any time in Android settings.
  • You can choose whether to show remote images.
  • You can turn diagnostic logging on or off in the app’s settings.
  • You can remove your account from the app, or uninstall the app, to delete its data from your device.

To ask about, access, correct or delete personal information related to your use of Qmail, contact info@qistarah.com. Because your work email account is provided and managed by Qistarah Tech, some requests, such as deleting a mailbox on the server, are handled under Qistarah Tech’s internal policies and any legal obligations that apply.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes or when the IMAP security limitation described in section 6 is resolved. The updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
